Science Brief

Permafrost

Permafrost is ground that remains frozen for long periods. Thaw can destabilize soil and release gases produced by decomposing organic matter.

Methane

Methane is a powerful greenhouse gas. In this simulation, pressure represents accumulated gas requiring cooling and containment.

Biological Risk

The pathogen layer is a fictionalized containment challenge. Sensors reveal uncertainty; seals and cooling reduce simulated growth.

Systems Engineering

A resilient vault balances energy, coolant, redundancy, monitoring, maintenance, and the ability to isolate local failures.
