How to Play

  1. Press Start. Balloons float left ➜ right.
  2. Drag the Shield to block hazards. The shield never harms balloons. Time slows slightly while dragging, and shield energy recharges when released.
  3. Use the Wind Fan: hold on the field to charge, then release. Use ← ↑ ↓ → to aim the gust. Blow against the route to slow objects.
  4. Helper Drone regroups balloons (capacity & target in Settings) and clears hazards around them.
  5. Bubble gives nearby balloons a temporary colored shield. Choose your color in Settings.
  6. The AI Director and progressive ramp gradually increase pressure. Deliver as many balloons as possible before the escort ends.
  7. Space pauses or resumes. R starts a new run. The game also pauses when the tab is hidden.
