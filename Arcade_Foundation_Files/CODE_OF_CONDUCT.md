# Community Code of Conduct

## Our Standard

Arcade™ welcomes thoughtful participation from artists, educators, learners, families, developers, and community members.

Participants are expected to:

- communicate with patience and respect;
- offer specific, constructive feedback;
- protect children's privacy;
- respect creative ownership and attribution;
- avoid harassment, intimidation, discrimination, and personal attacks;
- avoid publishing private information;
- keep technical discussions focused on the work;
- make room for different abilities, cultures, backgrounds, and levels of experience.

## Unacceptable Conduct

Unacceptable behavior includes:

- threats or targeted harassment;
- sexualized conduct directed at another participant;
- hateful or demeaning language;
- deliberate disruption;
- impersonation;
- publishing private information without permission;
- submitting malicious code;
- pressuring children or vulnerable users for contact or personal information;
- repeated disregard for project boundaries.

## Scope

This code applies to project repositories, issues, pull requests, discussions, and public spaces where someone is representing Arcade™ or Verve N Veda.

## Reporting

Report serious concerns privately through an established Verve N Veda contact channel.

Include enough information to understand the concern, but do not circulate private or sensitive material more widely than necessary.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit, hide, reject, or remove contributions and may limit participation when conduct threatens safety, dignity, privacy, or the integrity of the project.
