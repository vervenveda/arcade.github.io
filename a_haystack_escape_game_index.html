Haystack Escape Guide

Stable Level Mazes

Each campaign level has a fixed seed. Best times and move records are now measured against the same maze every time.

Three Stars

One star is awarded for escaping, one for collecting every grain bundle, and one for meeting the exact calculated footwork target.

Keys

Lantern Barn and later worlds may lock the exit. Find the visible key before entering the lantern gate.

Burrows

Paired burrows appear from Moonlit Orchard onward. Entering one carries the traveler to its partner without adding an extra move.

Wind

Scarecrow Hollow introduces gust tiles. A gust pushes in the direction of travel when the next cell is open.

Crows

Wandering crows move after each successful player step. Contact never blocks the maze; it adds a five-second detour penalty.

Deep Fog

Starfall Farm hides distant cells. The traveler can still move normally, and a Farm Lantern reveals the whole field temporarily.

Fair Coins

First clears pay a base reward. Raising a level’s best star rating grants only the difference, preventing repetitive coin farming.

Controls

Use arrows, WASD, swipes, or the direction pad. R restarts, P pauses, and Escape pauses an active maze.

Local Safety

Progress imports are size-limited and normalized. An original V1 save can be migrated without deleting its old storage entry.
