YardWorks Guide

Rotary Mower · 1

Fast broad mowing. Its deck cuts a full circular footprint rather than only one tile, but the body needs more turning space.

String Trimmer · 2

Narrow collision radius and edge-reaching cutting head. Use it between obstacles, along walls, and around garden beds.

Leaf Blower · 3

Hold the tool action while facing leaves. Leaves inside the air cone are collected for bonus points.

Garden Rake · 4

Use close to branches, sticks, and storm debris. The rake clears solid debris that the mower should avoid.

Movement

Arrow keys or WASD move. Diagonal speed is normalized. Space uses the current hand tool. P pauses.

Planner

Drag objects, edit exact coordinates and dimensions, validate reachability, and use Auto Space to repair tight lanes.
