Critter Crafter Guide

Two to Four Ingredients

Recipes stay readable and strategic. Choosing a fifth ingredient asks you to remove one first.

Stable Species

The sorted ingredient recipe and temperament lens create a deterministic species. The same recipe always returns the same identity.

Harmony

Complementary pairs increase Harmony. Opposing pairs create Paradox, raising weirdness and unusual powers rather than blocking the craft.

Discovery Rewards

A new species grants 12 Spark Dust. Re-crafting a known species grants 3 Dust and 6 XP only once per local calendar day.

Care

Train grants experience. Snacks raise Bond. Play raises Joy. All care changes are deliberate and saved locally.

Evolution

Experience determines level and form: Hatchling, Companion, Guardian, and Elder. Portraits and names visibly evolve.

Habitats

Assign any discovered critter to Meadow, Tidal Pool, Ember Den, Sky Nest, Moon Cave, or Crystal Glen.

Daily Research

One deterministic local-date challenge rewards 10 Spark Dust and cannot be repeatedly claimed.

Keyboard

C crafts, X clears, F favorites the current critter, and Ctrl/Cmd+K focuses Bestiary search.

Safe Local Data

Imports are bounded, normalized, and validated. The former V2 bestiary can be migrated when present.
