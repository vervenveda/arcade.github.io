Mind Garden Guide

Plant · 1

Place the selected species or garden feature on an empty tile. Living plants begin as young sprouts.

Water · 2

Restores water to a living plant and lightly waters its orthogonal neighbors.

Compost · 3

Improves plant health and gives the next growth check a small advantage.

Clean · 4

Removes daily litter and records progress toward cleanup challenges.

Prune · 5

Removes an existing plant or feature without creating an artificial score loop.

Inspect · 6

Opens health, water, stage, and visitor details without changing the plot.

Garden Day · N

Weather changes, plants consume water, healthy plants grow, and visitors redistribute.

Undo / Redo

Ctrl/Cmd+Z and Ctrl/Cmd+Y work alongside the visible buttons. One drag stroke is one undo step.

Stewardship

The score is recalculated from living garden quality, biodiversity, visitors, cleanliness, and completed challenges, preventing repeat-action farming.

Snapshots

Save up to twelve restorable plot memories. Full Data preserves the entire conservatory.
